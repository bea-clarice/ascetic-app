Guide

How Ascetic works

  • Log yesterday's screen time, study, sleep, exercise, and caffeine.
  • Ascetic estimates today's focus score and projected passing rate from that full-day pattern.
  • If focus drops into Digital Detox, start the 25-minute clock and finish the offline checklist.
  • Use Statistics to compare habits across days and review one AI nudge below the graphs.
